OceanCore document design and bank-details update

1. Extract this small ZIP and upload admin.html and the assets folder to your GitHub repository root. Preserve the subfolders. Commit the changes. No SQL changes are required for this update; it uses the existing billing setup.
2. Reload admin.html with Ctrl+Shift+R. Open Billing > Company Details. The restored ADCB information is pre-filled whenever your saved bank fields are blank. Verify the information and click Save Company Details so new documents store the values in their snapshot.
3. Print one invoice, quotation and delivery note. Choose A4 and turn browser headers/footers off. Enable background graphics if your browser omits the navy/blue fills.
